# Cority

> Enterprise Environmental, Health, Safety, and Quality (EHSQ) and sustainability software. Cority's CorityOne platform converges occupational health, safety and incident management, industrial hygiene, environmental and ESG/sustainability reporting, quality, and audit/compliance management for 1,500+ enterprise organizations, layered with Cortex AI agents. RESTful integration is provided through the Cority Enviance API under a Connectors license.
